# EDEN Financial Integrity Guardian

Status: planned module, not connected to banking systems.

## Purpose
Detect and investigate insider financial abuse without assuming that anomalies prove guilt.

## Controls
- Separation of duties: payment initiation, approval, execution, and reconciliation are distinct roles.
- Independently reconcile accounting entries with authorized bank and tax records.
- Flag beneficiary changes, unusual payment timing, repeated small payments, conflicting approvals and unexplained variances.
- Store tamper-evident audit events with independent retention and restricted administrator access.
- Route serious alerts to independent auditors or designated board-level reviewers.
- Preserve evidence and require human review before adverse employment actions.
- Support retrospective analysis of lawfully retained historical records.
- Minimize personal data and enforce role-based access to investigations.

## Implementation prerequisites
Authorized financial integrations, event normalization, evidence retention, audit controls, anomaly evaluation, independent security testing and human review.
